# Baked Feta Pasta

Inspired by the viral TikTok trend, this incredibly easy and comforting pasta dish features sweet cherry tomatoes and rich, creamy Greek feta baked together with olive oil, garlic, and chili flakes. The mixture is then tossed with hot pasta, starchy pasta water, and fresh basil to create a luscious, emulsified sauce.

- **Source:** [Original Recipe](https://youtu.be/U3gvRjIQ6Lo?si=BHPRMikl-g7iAbp7)

## Ingredients


### For the Bake

- 1 pound cherry tomatoes
- 6 to 8 ounces block Greek sheep's milk feta cheese
- 4 cloves garlic, split in half
- 1/3 to 1/2 cup extra-virgin olive oil
- Chili flakes, to taste
- Freshly cracked black pepper, to taste

### For the Pasta and Assembly

- 8 ounces (250g) pasta of your choice
- Kosher salt, for the pasta water
- 1 large handful fresh basil, roughly chopped
- Reserved starchy pasta water (about 1/4 to 1/2 cup)

## Instructions


### Prep and Bake

1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. Place the block of feta cheese in the center of a baking dish. Surround the feta with the cherry tomatoes and halved garlic cloves.
3. Pour the olive oil generously over the tomatoes, garlic, and feta. Season the dish with chili flakes and freshly cracked black pepper. Do not add extra salt, as the feta contains plenty of salt.
4. Bake for about 40 minutes, or until the tomatoes have burst and released their juices, and the feta is softened and lightly browned on top.

### Cook the Pasta

1. While the tomatoes and feta are baking, bring a pot of salted water to a boil and cook the pasta until al dente.
2. Just before draining, reserve about 1/2 cup of the starchy pasta water. Drain the pasta and set aside.

### Assemble and Emulsify

1. Remove the baking dish from the oven. Immediately add about three-quarters of the chopped fresh basil into the hot dish so it can wilt and infuse the sauce.
2. Pour the drained pasta into the baking dish, followed by about 1/4 cup of the reserved starchy pasta water.
3. Stir everything together vigorously. Mash the softened feta and pop the roasted tomatoes as you stir. Keep mixing until the oil, tomato juices, cheese, and pasta water emulsify into a smooth, creamy sauce that completely coats the pasta. If the sauce is too thick, add a splash more of the reserved pasta water.
4. Garnish with the remaining fresh basil and serve immediately.
