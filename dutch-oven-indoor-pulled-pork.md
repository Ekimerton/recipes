# Dutch Oven Indoor Pulled Pork

A versatile, tender, and highly economical indoor pulled pork made in a Dutch oven. Seasoned with a robust spice rub and braised with a splash of whiskey, this pork shoulder cooks low and slow until it is tender enough to shred with a fork. It serves as an excellent, flavorful base for sandwiches, tacos, pasta, quesadillas, and more.

- **Source:** [Original Recipe](https://youtu.be/-07gtGrdeyE?is=dnk6Wjr64LzvJYHf)

## Ingredients


### Spice Rub

- Brown sugar
- Kosher salt
- Black pepper
- Paprika
- Cayenne pepper
- Cumin
- Coriander
- Fennel seed
- Mexican oregano
- (Note: You can substitute the homemade rub with your favorite high-quality, pre-made store-bought barbecue rub).*

### Pork and Braising

- 1 bone-in pork shoulder (Boston butt), 5 to 10 pounds
- 1 to 2 tablespoons neutral cooking oil
- 1 splash of whiskey (such as bourbon or rye)

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 300°F (150°C).
2. Generously apply the spice rub all over the pork shoulder, making sure to rub it into all the cracks, crevices, and edges.
3. Heat a large Dutch oven on the stovetop over low heat to preheat, then increase the heat to high. Add a splash of cooking oil to the bottom of the pot.
4. Place the pork shoulder into the Dutch oven (fat/skin side down first) and sear on all sides until a nice brown crust develops. Because the rub contains sugar, it will brown quickly; the entire searing process should take about 5 to 10 minutes total.
5. Add a splash of whiskey to the pot to deglaze. Let the alcohol cook off (optionally, you can carefully ignite the alcohol and let the flame burn out naturally).
6. Place the lid on the Dutch oven and transfer it to the preheated oven.
7. Cook covered until the meat is tender. For a 5-to-7-pound shoulder, cook for about 4 hours; for a larger 10-pound shoulder, cook for 5 hours or more. The pork is ready when a knife slides in with very little resistance and the bone slides out easily.
8. Remove the lid and return the Dutch oven to the oven uncovered for another 45 to 60 minutes to allow a crisp bark to develop on the exterior.
9. Remove from the oven and carefully slide the bone out of the meat.
10. Using two forks, roughly shred the pork directly inside the Dutch oven, mixing the shredded meat back into its rendered juices so it stays moist as it cools.
