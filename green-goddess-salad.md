# Green Goddess Salad
For 2 servings

## Ingredients
Salad
- A few large kale leaves
- 1 baby cucumber
- 2 scallions, light green & white parts
- 1 avocado
- Feta cheese to taste
- Optional: chicken

Dressing
- 1/4 cup basil leaves or basil paste equivalent 
- 1/2 cup fresh spinach
- 1 clove garlic
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 1/8 cup olive oil
- 1/8 cup raw, unsalted cashews
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
- 2 scallions, dark green tips

## Instructions

1. Finely chop the kale, cucumbers, green onions & avocado and add to a large bowl.
2. Add the dressing ingredients to a high-powered blender in the order listed (liquids should go in first). Use whatever parts of the scallions you didn’t toss in the salad. Pulse until the dressing is completely smooth and no green specks remain.
3. Pour the dressing over the salad and mix well to evenly coat. Serve immediately.
