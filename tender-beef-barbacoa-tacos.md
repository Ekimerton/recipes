# Beef Barbacoa

This incredibly juicy and flavor-packed beef barbacoa is slow-braised to meltingly tender perfection. Inspired by the beloved Mexican classic, this recipe uses dried ancho chiles, smoky chipotles in adobo, and a secret splash of fish sauce to build a rich, deeply savory flavor profile that easily surpasses any restaurant version.

- **Source:** [Original Recipe](https://www.seriouseats.com/tender-beef-barbacoa-chipotle-tacos-recipe)

## Ingredients

### Chili and Aromatics Base

- 1 pack of dried ancho chiles (3-4 chiles), stems and seeds removed
- 4 cups of low-sodium chicken broth, split
- 1 tablespoon of neutral cooking oil, such as canola or vegetable
- 1 small white or yellow onion, thinly sliced
- 6 cloves of garlic, peeled and lightly crushed
- 2 teaspoons of cumin powder
- 2 teaspoons of dried oregano (preferably Mexican oregano)
- 1/2 teaspoon of ground cloves
- 4 chipotle peppers in adobo sauce, chopped, plus 2 tablespoons of the adobo sauce from the can
- 1/4 cup of apple cider vinegar
- 2 teaspoons of fish sauce

### Meat and Braising

- 4 pounds of beef chuck eye roast (or boneless short ribs)
- Kosher salt and freshly cracked black pepper, to taste
- 2 dried bay leaves

## Instructions

1. Adjust your oven rack to the lower-middle position and preheat the oven to 275°F (135°C).
2. Place the dried ancho chiles in the bottom of a large, heavy-bottomed Dutch oven and set it over medium-high heat. Cook the chiles, flipping them occasionally with tongs, for 2 to 3 minutes until they are highly fragrant and slightly darkened. Move the toasted chiles to a bowl, pour in 2 cups of the chicken stock, cover, and microwave on high for about 5 minutes (or simmer on the stove) until completely softened.
3. In the empty Dutch oven, heat the 1 tablespoon of oil over medium heat. Stir in the sliced onions and crushed garlic, cooking and stirring frequently for about 10 minutes until they are a deep, rich brown and beginning to char.
4. Add the ground cumin, oregano, and ground cloves to the pot. Cook, stirring continuously, for about 30 seconds until the spices are aromatic.
5. Add the chopped chipotles, adobo sauce, apple cider vinegar, and the remaining 2 cups of chicken stock. Stir well while scraping up all the browned, caramelized bits from the bottom of the pot.
6. Carefully transfer the contents of the Dutch oven into a blender jar. Add the softened dried chiles along with their soaking liquid and the fish sauce. Cover securely, start the blender on low speed, and gradually increase to high. Puree until the mixture is completely velvety and smooth, about 1 minute.
7. Arrange the beef chuck roast (or short ribs) and bay leaves inside the Dutch oven. Season generously with kosher salt and black pepper, then pour the pureed chili sauce over everything.
8. Bring the mixture to a boil over high heat on the stovetop. Once boiling, cover the pot with its lid slightly cracked, and transfer it to the preheated oven. Cook, turning the beef roast occasionally, for roughly 4 hours, or until the meat is incredibly tender and yields easily with no resistance when poked.
9. Remove the pot from the oven. Carefully discard the bay leaves. Transfer the chuck roast to a large platter.
10. Set the Dutch oven on the stove over medium-high heat. Boil the remaining braising liquid, stirring often, until it has reduced to about 1 1/2 cups, which should take around 5 minutes.
11. Use two forks or your fingers to shred the beef into bite-sized chunks. Toss the shredded meat back into the pot with the reduced braising sauce until well coated.
12. Serve the warm barbacoa beef immediately, or for the absolute best flavor, store the shredded beef and reduced liquid in a sealed container in the refrigerator for up to 5 days before reheating.
