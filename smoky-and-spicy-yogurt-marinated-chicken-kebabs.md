# Smoky and Spicy Yogurt-Marinated Chicken

These flavorful, juicy grilled chicken thighs feature a rich, smoky, and spicy marinade inspired by traditional Indian tandoori chicken. Whole milk yogurt acts as a gentle tenderizer to ensure the boneless chicken thighs stay incredibly moist on the hot grill, while hot and smoked paprikas deliver a deep, complex warmth.

- **Source:** [Original Recipe](https://www.seriouseats.com/smoky-spicy-yogurt-marinated-chicken-kebabs-recipe)

## Ingredients

- 1 cup plain unsweetened yogurt
- 1/4 cup fresh lemon juice (from about 2 lemons)
- 3 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 tablespoon peeled and finely grated fresh ginger
- 1 tablespoon kosher salt
- 2 teaspoons smoked paprika
- 2 teaspoons hot paprika
- 2 teaspoons freshly minced garlic (about 2 medium cloves)
- 1 teaspoon freshly cracked black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 2 pounds skinless, boneless chicken thighs

## Instructions

1. In a medium mixing bowl, thoroughly combine the plain yogurt, lemon juice, extra-virgin olive oil, grated ginger, kosher salt, smoked paprika, hot paprika, minced garlic, black pepper, cumin, and cayenne pepper.
2. Put the chicken thighs inside a large, resealable zip-top plastic bag. Pour the spiced yogurt marinade over the meat, seal the bag while removing as much air as possible, and refrigerate. Let the chicken marinate for 4 to 8 hours.
3. Fill a charcoal chimney with charcoal and light it. When the coals are fully ignited and covered in gray ash, pour and spread them in a flat, even layer across the bottom coal grate. Place the cooking grate on top, close the grill lid, and let it preheat for 5 minutes. Clean and oil the hot cooking grates.
4. Remove the chicken from the marinade, allowing any excess to drip off. Position the chicken thighs on the grill grates. Cook, turning occasionally, until the chicken is beautifully charred and browned. The internal temperature at the center of the thickest part of the meat should register between 160°F and 165°F (71°C to 74°C) on a digital instant-read thermometer, taking about 5 to 7 minutes on each side.
5. Transfer the grilled chicken to a clean platter. Let the meat rest for 5 minutes, then serve immediately.
